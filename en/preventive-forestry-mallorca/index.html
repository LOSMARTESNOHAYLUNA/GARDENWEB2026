What preventive forestry is in Mallorca

Preventive forestry consists of managing the vegetation on woodland or mixed plots to reduce the risk of fire. In the Balearic Islands there are specific regulations requiring owners of wooded land to maintain safety strips and biomass-management areas. Failure to comply can lead to fines and, in the event of a fire, civil liability.

Management and reduction of combustible biomass on woodland plots

Creation and maintenance of safety strips

Removal of high-risk dry vegetation

Pruning of pines and trees to raise the forest canopy

Documentation of the work carried out to prove compliance

Work on estates of any size and topography

The difference between preventive forestry and estate clearing

Preventive forestry has a specific aim regulated by law: reducing the risk of fire in woodland areas or the wildland-urban interface. It is not simply a cosmetic clear-up: the work follows specific technical forest-management criteria and may be necessary to meet the owner's legal obligations. If your estate is in woodland or borders woodland, talk to us about your prevention obligations too.
