Who we are

One team, one island, one standard of care.

We are Garden Solution Mallorca, a family-run gardening company with more than ten years caring for villas, estates and communities across the island. Our in-house team combines technical expertise —with an official phytosanitary licence— with a close, personal way of working that our clients value season after season.

We work with the belief that a well-kept garden is an extension of the home. That is why every visit counts, every pruning has a purpose, and every landscaping project starts by listening to the people who will live in it.

— Gabriel and Eloy, founding partners

Frequently asked questions

What you ask us

The most common questions from our clients. If you have any others, write to us and we'll reply within 24h.

Which areas of Mallorca do you cover?

We cover the whole of Mallorca except Manacor. Our main areas are Palma, Llucmajor, Marratxí, Calvià and Alcúdia. We have also carried out projects in Esporles, Valldemossa and Caimari.

How much does garden maintenance cost?

Every garden is unique, which is why we prefer to make a free technical visit before giving you a quote tailored to your real needs.

Do you work with residents' associations?

Yes, although our speciality is luxury private properties, where we can apply all our experience in premium gardening.

How many years of experience do you have?

We have over 10 years of experience in gardening in Mallorca and a current portfolio of 40 recurring regular clients.

Can you use phytosanitary products?

Yes. We hold an official phytosanitary licence, which allows us to apply regulated chemical treatments legally and safely, as well as a pool maintenance licence.

Do you treat the red palm weevil?

Yes, we treat the red palm weevil with endotherapy and preventive protocols. It is one of our most requested services among palm tree owners in Mallorca.

How long does a technical visit take?

We usually schedule the technical visit within 48-72h of your request. It is free and with no obligation.

Do you also carry out garden design projects?

Yes. Landscaping projects are one of our core services: we design and build complete gardens, from concept to planting and irrigation systems.
