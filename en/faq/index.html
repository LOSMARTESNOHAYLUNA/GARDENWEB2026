Frequently asked questions

The most common questions from owners of villas, estates and communities about our gardening services in Mallorca. If you can't find your answer here, write to us or call 621 49 39 77.

We cover the whole of Mallorca except Manacor. Our main service areas are Palma, Llucmajor, Marratxí, Calvià and Alcúdia. We have also carried out projects in Esporles, Valldemossa and Caimari. If your property is in another municipality, ask about availability with no obligation.

We don't publish generic prices because every garden is different: it depends on the size, the type of plants, the frequency of visits and the garden's current condition. The technical visit is free and with no obligation — from there we draw up a fixed, tailored quote.

Yes, it's one of our most common client profiles. Second-home owners who spend part of the year away entrust us with the ongoing care of their garden and pool. We coordinate by phone or email and the property is always in perfect condition when the owner arrives.

Over 10 years of experience in gardening in Mallorca, with a current client base of more than 40 regular repeat clients. It's the result of years of steady work and a way of doing things that our clients value season after season.

All the work is carried out by our own in-house team. We never subcontract. The same team that speaks with you is the one that works in your garden, which guarantees consistent quality on every visit and an accumulated knowledge of your property that cannot be subcontracted.

Yes. We hold an official phytosanitary licence, which authorises us to apply regulated phytosanitary products legally and safely. It's an essential requirement for treatments such as endotherapy against the red palm weevil, and not every gardening service has one.

Yes. We treat the red palm weevil using plant endotherapy — injecting the product directly into the palm tree's vascular system. It's the most effective method available. We also carry out preventive protocols for healthy palm trees in areas with a high incidence of the pest.

Our guide prices are: a palm tree pruned annually, from €50 – €70; a palm tree pruned every two years, from €80 – €100. The exact price depends on the size, the species and the access. Send photos or request a visit for an accurate quote.

Yes. Landscaping projects are one of our main services. We design and build complete gardens, from the initial concept through to planting, automatic irrigation and decorative features. We work on both new projects and redesigns of existing gardens.

It depends on the species. In general, late winter and early spring are the best times for most trees, when they are dormant. Palm trees can be pruned throughout the year. During the technical visit we'll tell you the best time for each species in your garden.

Yes. We design and install automatic irrigation systems in both new and existing gardens. We create a tailored design according to the different areas of the garden, install pipes, sprinklers or drippers to suit each area, and program the controller so it runs on its own.

Yes. We hold an official pool maintenance licence, which authorises us to manage the water treatment of private pools with regulated products at the correct doses. We offer pool maintenance either on its own or combined with the gardening service.

The technical visit usually takes place within 24-72 hours of the request, depending on the area and availability. It lasts between 30 and 60 minutes depending on the size of the property. It is completely free and with no obligation.

Yes. We carry out clearing, plot tidying and preventive forestry work on estates of any size. It's one of our most requested services in municipalities such as Llucmajor, where large rural plots with dense vegetation are common.

Yes. We offer full service in Spanish, English and German. This is especially useful for international villa owners in areas such as Calvià or Alcúdia, where there is a strong presence of British and German residents.

Yes, although our speciality is luxury private properties. We also work with residents' communities in Mallorca, especially developments with shared green areas. Each case is quoted according to the surface area and the frequency of visits required.

Yes. We install artificial grass on any surface: soil, concrete or wood. It's a particularly popular solution in Mallorca given the dry summer, as artificial grass stays green without irrigation during the hottest months.
