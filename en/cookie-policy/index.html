1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites store in the user's browser when they access them. They allow the site to remember information about the visit, such as the preferred language or browsing preferences, which makes future visits easier and the site more useful.

These are cookies strictly necessary for the basic operation of the website. Without them, the site cannot function correctly. They do not require the user's consent under the applicable regulations.

When you first access the website, a banner appears allowing you to accept or reject non-technical cookies. This preference is stored for one year and can be changed at any time from your browser settings.

You can also manage or delete cookies from your browser settings:

  • Microsoft Edge

Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the correct operation of the website.
